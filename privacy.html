I Fits, I Sits — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

I Fits, I Sits: Cat Jump ("the game") is made by Adam Goodwin ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

  • The game does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • No accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, cats and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • When you're online, the game checks this website for game updates. Nothing about you or your progress is sent.

Information the game stores on your device

The levels you've finished, your stars, the cats you've met and your sound and vibration settings are stored locally using standard iOS storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Game updates

Now and then the game checks this website for an update to the game and downloads it. That request contains no personal information and no identifiers. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, can see the IP address a request comes from; we don't receive or store it.

Purchases

The first world is free. The other worlds are one purchase, "The Whole Game", sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game only whether you own it. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Ratings

Once in a while the game may show Apple's own "enjoying it?" rating box. Whether it appears, and what you choose, is handled by Apple; the game doesn't see your answer.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences. It is not in the App Store's Kids category. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and shows no ads.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy at this address.
