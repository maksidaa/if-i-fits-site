If I Fits — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

If I Fits: Cat Jump ("the game") is made by Adam Goodwin ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

  • No accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your progress (scores, lives, unlocked cats, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional video ads, provided by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one to continue a run. Ads are requested as non-personalized: we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and the game never asks for permission to do so.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center if you're signed in to it.

Information the game stores on your device

Scores, salami, lives, unlocked cats, the to-do list, daily results and sound settings are stored locally using standard iOS storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you tap "watch a video to continue", the game loads an ad from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's software may collect:

  • your IP address (to estimate an approximate location),
  • device and app information, including an app-specific device identifier,
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown, taps, video views),
  • diagnostic data (crash and performance information).

We configure AdMob to serve non-personalized ads and to limit ad content to a parental-guidance rating. The game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, so Google cannot use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) through this game. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message lets you choose how your data is used; you can change that choice any time in Settings → ad privacy choices.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional Cat Pack is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game only whether you own the pack. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and it is not in the App Store's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a parental-guidance content rating and are non-personalized.

Your choices

  • Don't want ads? Never tap "watch a video to continue"; the game never shows ads otherwise. The Cat Pack also lets you continue without videos.
  • Change ad privacy choices (where offered) in Settings → ad privacy choices.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy at this address.
