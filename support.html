Help with I Fits, I Sits: Cat Jump for iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

How do I play?

Touch anywhere and pull back to aim, like a slingshot. The dotted line shows where the cat will go; let go to fling it. Knock things over and land a cat in every spot (the dashed boxes) to win. While a cat is flying, or just after it lands, tap anywhere to do its trick.

What do the stars mean?

Each level has up to three stars: one for sitting a cat in every spot, one for having enough cats left over, and one for breaking enough stuff. The goals are on the card before each level. Bonus levels give stars for how much you break.

How do I open more levels and worlds?

Win a level to open the next one. Each new world opens once you've earned enough stars in the levels before it; the level list shows how many you need. Go back to earlier levels for more stars at any time.

I'm stuck on a level

After a miss you can ask for one more cat. Lose a level twice and paw prints show one way to win, so every level can be beaten. Tap the round arrow at the top to start a level over. Sometimes a wild idea works too: bounce off a wall, knock something into place, or call megachonk.

Who is megachonk?

A very large cat who arrives in his own cat-a-pult. From kitchen 4 on, tap his button while you aim to call him once per level. He costs some of your saved-up money (the hooman's bill), and everything he breaks counts. Sometimes he's exactly what a level needs. Sometimes he sits on the wrong box.

What is the hooman's bill? What's the closet?

Everything the cats break adds to the hooman's bill. Spend it in the closet on hats, collars and outfits for each cat, or on megachonk. Tap the "saved" money in a level, or the shop button after a level, to go shopping.

How do I restore The Whole Game?

On the level list, tap restore purchase, or tap any locked level and choose restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. The Whole Game supports Family Sharing.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the pause button at the top left during a level. You can switch sound, room sounds and vibration on or off there, and choose a short or full aim line. The game plays sound even when your iPhone is set to silent, so use the sound switch in the game.

Does my progress sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Progress, stars and closet items are saved on your iPhone or iPad, and deleting the game deletes them. Your purchase of The Whole Game always restores.

Does it need the internet? Are there ads?

No and no. The game plays fully offline, with no ads and no tracking. When you're online it checks this website for signed updates to the game: fixes and new levels for the same game.
