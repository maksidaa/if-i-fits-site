Help with If I Fits: Cat Jump for iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

How do I play?

Touch and hold anywhere on the playfield to wind up, then let go to leap. The longer you hold, the farther the cat jumps. Land inside the next thing to score; land right in the middle for a perfect fit.

How do I restore the Cat Pack?

Tap the paw (cats) button on the home screen and tap restore purchases under the Cat Pack, or open Settings (the gear) and tap restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. The Cat Pack supports Family Sharing.

How do continues work?

After a miss, you can continue the run once: spend one of your nine lives, watch an optional short video, or (with the Cat Pack) continue for free. Continues aren't available in today's house, and runs with a continue don't go on the leaderboards.

How do I get more lives?

Collect salami slices along the jumps and finish to-do items. Every 25 slices earns a life, up to nine.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play if you tap "watch a video to continue". The game never shows ads otherwise.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires a choice (for example in the EU and UK), open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap ad privacy choices.

Where are the leaderboards?

Tap ranks on the home screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings.

Does my progress sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Progress is saved on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the game deletes it.
